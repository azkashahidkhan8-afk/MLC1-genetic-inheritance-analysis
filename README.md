# MLC1 Exon 3 Screening and Genetic Inheritance Analysis in Non-Syndromic Intellectual Disability

## 1. Project Overview

This repository documents my postgraduate research conducted as part of the MS Zoology (Human Genetics) programme at Lahore College for Women University (LCWU), Lahore, Pakistan.

The study investigated the autosomal recessive inheritance pattern of *MLC1* gene mutations in selected families affected by non-syndromic intellectual disability. The research focused on screening exon 3 of the *MLC1* gene, examining family pedigrees and interpreting sequence data to investigate the potential involvement of *MLC1* in the studied families.

The project combined family-based genetic investigation, molecular laboratory techniques and sequence interpretation.

## 2. Research Background

Intellectual disability is a neurodevelopmental condition characterized by limitations in intellectual functioning and adaptive behaviour. Genetic factors can contribute to its occurrence, and studying inheritance patterns within affected families can help investigate the possible genetic basis of the condition.

Autosomal recessive inheritance is an important pattern to consider in families with affected individuals, particularly when family histories and pedigree structures are consistent with recessive transmission.

This research focused on *MLC1*, a gene located on chromosome 22q13.33, and investigated exon 3 in selected families with non-syndromic intellectual disability.

## 5. Research Methodology

### 5.1 Family Visits and Pedigree Assessment

Family visits were conducted to collect relevant family information and blood samples from affected and unaffected family members.

Pedigrees were constructed to examine relationships among family members and investigate inheritance patterns consistent with autosomal recessive transmission.

### 5.2 Genomic DNA Extraction

Genomic DNA was extracted from collected blood samples using the phenol-chloroform-isoamyl alcohol (PCI) method.

### 5.3 DNA Quality Assessment

Extracted DNA quality was assessed using agarose gel electrophoresis to examine DNA integrity and suitability for downstream molecular analysis.

### 5.4 Primer Design and Evaluation

Forward and reverse primers targeting *MLC1* exon 3 were designed and evaluated using:

- Primer3
- NCBI Primer-BLAST
- IDT OligoAnalyzer

These tools supported primer selection and evaluation of primer characteristics for the target region.

### 5.5 PCR Optimization and Amplification

Conventional polymerase chain reaction (PCR) conditions were optimized for amplification of *MLC1* exon 3.

Agarose gel electrophoresis was used to assess the amplification products.

### 5.6 Sanger Sequencing and Chromatogram Interpretation

PCR-amplified products were submitted for external Sanger sequencing.

The resulting sequencing chromatograms were interpreted using Chromas. The sequencing procedure itself was performed externally; my contribution involved interpretation of the returned sequence data.

### 5.7 Reference Sequence Comparison

NCBI BLAST and Ensembl were used to access reference information and support sequence comparison and interpretation of the targeted *MLC1* region.

The analysis remained focused on the selected exon of *MLC1* rather than genome-wide or multi-gene screening.
